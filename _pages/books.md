## Books

### **Globalization, Poverty, and Income Inequality: Insights from Indonesia**  
Barichello, Richard; Patunru, Arianto A.; and Schwindt, Richard (Eds).  
Vancouver: UBC Press, 2021.  
ISBN: 978-077-4865-61-6.
Links: [UBC Press](https://www.ubcpress.ca/globalization-poverty-and-income-inequality)

An edited volume examining the relationships between globalization, poverty, and income inequality in Indonesia, bringing together empirical and policy-oriented contributions.

**Reviews:**

- Durongkaveroj, Wannaphong (2022), *Asian-Pacific Economic Literature* 36(1): 143–145.  
- Booth, Anne (2022), *Bulletin of Indonesian Economic Studies* 58(3): 335–337.
